# BonaFlow — the two-minute play

**Cast:** Vijay = hungry guest · Martin = operator/narrator
**Props:** two phones (guest + staff), projector mirroring the guest phone, one real bowl
**Total: 2:10.** Rehearse twice. The beats matter more than the words.

---

### Beat 0 — before you speak (0:00)

QR is already on screen. Martin, to the room:

> **"Scan that. You're now a guest at this event — the same one you had lunch at."**

Let them scan while Beat 1 runs. **Do not wait for them.**

---

### Beat 1 — the problem, played not described (0:10)

**Vijay** *(walks on, phone out, genuinely annoyed)*
> "I'm vegan. There are four counters. Which one has something I can actually eat?"

**Martin**
> "That's the question nobody can answer at a catered event. And forty metres away, the person behind Counter B knows the vegan bowls are nearly gone — and they're the only person who knows it."

*(beat)*

> **"The gap isn't sensing. It's distribution."**

---

### Beat 2 — the guest side (0:30)

**Vijay** *(taps Vegan on the guest phone — mirrored)*
> "Vegan. Counter B has the chickpea quinoa salad — but the queue's high. So it's sending me to Counter C instead."

**Martin**
> "Real dishes. Real allergens, transcribed off the printed bowl labels at lunch today. Mustard on that one — declared by the caterer, not guessed by us."

---

### Beat 3 — the live moment. Protect this. (0:50)

**Martin** *(picks up the second phone, staff view)*
> "Now I'm the person at Counter B."

*(speaks into it, plainly)*
> **"Vegan Chickpeas Quinoa Salad is almost finished, about twenty people waiting."**

*(taps Confirm on the extraction)*

> **"I'm not touching his phone."**

*(count silently to two — let the room's phones change too)*

**Vijay** *(looks down, genuinely)*
> "It just went red. And it moved me to Counter C."

**Martin**
> "Every phone in this room did the same thing. Three seconds."

---

### Beat 4 — the coffee (1:15)

**Vijay** *(eats a forkful, pushes the bowl away)*
> "Right. I want a coffee."

**Martin**
> "Rate the bowl. You get one free."

**Vijay** *(holds the mic button)*
> **"Really good, but way too much quinoa — I left half of it."**

*(taps Confirm)*

> "...and there's my coffee."

**Martin** *(to the room)*
> "That's the loop closing. He told us **why** he left food, in his own words, in nine seconds — and the kitchen gets it as structured data, not a star rating."

---

### Beat 5 — what it's worth (1:40)

**Martin** *(switches to Operations)*
> "Counter B: replenish, high priority, already queued. And underneath — what people actually left, and why.
>
> We don't count people with a camera. We don't guess the queue. **Your staff already know — we just move what they know to everyone who needs it.**
>
> The model reads. The rules decide. That's why it's auditable."

---

### Beat 6 — close (2:00)

> **"BonaFlow turns every catering station into a connected, responsive part of the event."**

*(stop talking)*

## If something breaks

| Fails | Do |
|---|---|
| Guest phone doesn't update | Long-press to fire the override. Say: *"parse is real, I'm firing the cached one."* Keep going. |
| Voice doesn't transcribe | Type it. Say: *"mic's fighting the room — same path."* |
| Everything | Backup video. Keep narrating over it. |

**Never apologise for a fallback.** Naming it out loud reads as competence.

---

## Q&A — the four that will come

**"You're paying for reviews. Doesn't that bias the data?"**
> "Yes, some. A coffee gets you volume, and volume skews to people who wanted to talk anyway. What it buys is the *reason* — and the reason is the thing a star rating never gives you. We'd rather have a biased sample of reasons than a clean sample of numbers. And it's the caterer's lever to turn off."

**"Do you detect queues?"**
> "No, and we don't claim to. Staff report it. That's honest and it's instant."

**"Is this affiliated with Bella&Bona?"**
> "No. Independent prototype. The dish names and allergens are transcribed from today's bowl labels; the counter layout is simulated."

**"What if the AI gets it wrong?"**
> "Nothing changes until a human confirms it. And we keep what was said separate from what the model inferred, each with a confidence value."

## Rules

- **No euro figures.** You can't substantiate one and a sharp founder will ask.
- **Say "cached" when it's cached.**
- Vijay's lines are short on purpose. If he improvises, Martin stays on script.
- **Beat 3 is the pitch.** If you're running long, cut Beat 5's middle paragraph, never Beat 3.
